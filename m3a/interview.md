---

### Tips while you interview

- Write down what he says, don't fix it or upgrade the wording.
- If an answer's too vague ("a long time," "a cool game"), you can ask one follow-up like "like 5 minutes or 20?" or "can you give me an example?" — but don't suggest the answer.
- E-03 (dislikes) is especially useful — it directly shapes your acceptance checks later.
- Do not argue with his answers, even if something sounds hard to build. That's a Part 1/4 problem (budget and cut list), not an interview problem.
